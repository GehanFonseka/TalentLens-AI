02 • Domain

The research landscape.

Literature survey, research gaps, problem statement, objectives, and five-tier microservice methodology.

In high-volume recruitment workflows, manual resume evaluation is labor-intensive and prone to cognitive bias. Traditional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) rely heavily on exact keyword matching, creating what researchers identify as the Lexical Screening Trap [1]. Candidates using synonymous terminology (e.g., "PostgreSQL" vs "relational databases") are routinely penalized.

Domain Area Pioneering Work Conventional Approach Identified Scientific Limit
Resume Parsing Zaroor et al. (2019) Regex & rule-based matching Fragile across dynamic multi-column layouts and tables.
Candidate Matching Devlin et al. (2019); Reimers (2019) Bi-Encoder cosine similarity Lacks token-level cross-attention; misses context subtleties.
Interview Evaluation Radford et al. (2022 Whisper) Uncalibrated rubric scoring High variance across human interviewers; uncalibrated ordinal tiers.
Turnover Prediction Prokhorenkova et al. (2018 CatBoost) Default τ=0.50 cutoff models Severe minority recall suppression under high class imbalance.

To resolve these limits, our research grounds itself in state-of-the-art architectures: Conditional Random Fields (CRF) for structured entity tagging, full sequence-pair BERT Cross-Encoders for semantic matching, Faster-Whisper with Platt-calibrated DeBERTa-v3 for interview grading, and Ordered CatBoost with TreeSHAP for retention forecasting.

Despite advancements in commercial HR tech, existing platforms suffer from four acute structural voids:

1. Semantic Disconnect

Screening algorithms evaluate surface keyword frequency rather than conceptual competency depth and demonstrated project proficiency.

2. Uncalibrated Oral Evaluation

Technical oral assessments lack automated transcription grounding and probabilistic calibration across multi-tier ordinal rubric scales.

3. Reactive Turnover Management

Turnover analysis operates exclusively post-hire through exit questionnaires rather than advisory pre-hire risk screening.

4. Monolithic Silos

Recruitment tools operate as disconnected point solutions, preventing holistic multi-factor candidate analysis under unified explainable AI.

How can an integrated, multimodal artificial intelligence ecosystem streamline technical talent acquisition by accurately extracting candidate proficiencies, computing deep semantic alignment, conducting calibrated oral assessments, and forecasting retention risk under strict human governance?

Key Research Constraints

  • • Class Imbalance: Mitigating extreme 1:5.20 negative-to-positive ratio in turnover records.
  • • Low-Latency Inference: Ensuring BERT Cross-Encoder scoring operates within sub-second constraints on commodity hardware via ONNX Runtime.
  • • Ethical AI Guardrails: Providing complete local TreeSHAP attributions with strictly zero autonomous hiring decisions.

Main Objective: To formulate, implement, and validate a unified multimodal AI architecture for technical talent acquisition and retention analytics that optimizes screening accuracy and operational efficiency.

Specific Sub-Objectives

Sub-Objective 1 (Chamudi Jayantha): Develop a multi-format parsing pipeline combining SpaCy and CRF entity recognition to extract skills, tenure, and education with heuristic quality regression.
Sub-Objective 2 (Kavishka Deshan): Engineer a 6-layer BERT Cross-Encoder on ONNX Runtime to evaluate full sequence-pair token interactions at operating cutoff τ=0.44.
Sub-Objective 3 (Sajani Tennakoon): Build a speech interview evaluation engine using Silero VAD, Faster-Whisper INT8 transcription, and Platt-calibrated DeBERTa-v3 ordinal grading.
Sub-Objective 4 (Gehan Fonseka): Train an Optuna-tuned Ordered CatBoost model across 52 tabular features with operating threshold τ=0.33, TreeSHAP attributions, and What-If simulation.

Our system is organized as a resilient Five-Tier Distributed Microservice Architecture composed of 12 decoupled Docker containers:

Tier Level Container Service Technology Stack Core Functionality
Tier 1: Presentation Web Dashboard (port 3000) React, Vite, CSS Interactive candidate portal and recruiter dashboard.
Tier 2: Gateway API Gateway (port 4000) Express, JWT, CORS Request routing, rate limiting, and centralized authentication.
Tier 3: Core Logic Recruitment Core (port 4001) Node.js, Express Candidate workflows, application states, audit logs.
Tier 4: AI Inference Matching Engine (port 4002) Node.js, ONNX Runtime 6-layer BERT Cross-Encoder scoring at τ=0.44.
Tier 4: AI Inference Interview Engine (port 4003) Python, Faster-Whisper INT8 audio transcription and DeBERTa-v3 rubric grading.
Tier 4: AI Inference Retention Engine (port 4004) Python, CatBoost, SHAP 52-feature turnover scoring at τ=0.33 and TreeSHAP.
Tier 5: Persistence MongoDB & Redis Mongo 7.0, Redis 7.2 Document datastore and in-memory caching.
